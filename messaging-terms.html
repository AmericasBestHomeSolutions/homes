Messaging Terms & Conditions

The terms of our text messaging program, in plain language.

Effective August 7, 2026 · Last updated August 7, 2026

Program description

Americas Best Home Solutions LLC sends text messages about property listings, showings, offers, transaction updates, and related follow-up to individuals who have opted in to receive them.

How to opt in

You may opt in by submitting a form on our website and checking the consent box, by giving verbal consent during a phone conversation with our team, or by replying to a request for consent by email.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your activity and interest.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge for the messages themselves; your mobile carrier's standard rates apply.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive a confirmation message and no further messages will be sent.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, email admin@americasbesthomesolutions.com, or call (551) 550-9906.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy at https://getownerfinancedhomes.com/privacy-policy.

Consent to receive text messages is never a condition of buying a home from us, selling a property to us, or getting a reply to any inquiry. You can leave the box unchecked and we will still respond.
