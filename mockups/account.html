How we got here. In a lot of American towns, the monthly cost of renting is higher than the monthly cost of owning the same house — and the only thing standing between a renting family and an owned home is the bank's approval process. We find homes in those towns whose owners will sell on terms, and connect them with the people who want to live in them.
