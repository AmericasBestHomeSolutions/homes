Straight answers

Is this the same as renting?

No. When you rent, the money is gone and the home stays the landlord's. Here, you're the buyer from day one and every payment goes toward owning the home at the price you agreed to.

Do I need a bank to buy one of these homes?

No — there's no bank and no mortgage application. The home's owner is the seller, so the purchase is agreed in a written contract between you and the owner. We market the home and guide you through the process. Call us and we'll talk through your situation honestly.

Can I paint, fix up, or improve the home?

Yes — it's your home. Many buyers love making it their own. We'll cover any specifics in your agreement.
