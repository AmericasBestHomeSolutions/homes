Is this a bank loan?

No. This is seller financing under a written agreement you review before you sign. No bank, no mortgage application.

Who owns the home?

Right now, the home is owned by the seller. Americas Best Home Solutions works as the seller's marketing specialist: we list the home, answer your questions, and guide you through every step. When your agreement is fulfilled, you're the new homeowner.

What condition is the home in?

Homes are sold as-is. Tour it in person and decide for yourself.
