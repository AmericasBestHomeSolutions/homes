Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Americas Best Home Solutions LLC collects when you use getownerfinancedhomes.com, what we do with it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Effective August 7, 2026 · Last updated August 7, 2026

Who we are

Americas Best Home Solutions LLC ("we", "us", "our") is a Wyoming limited liability company that sells residential property with owner financing and acquires property from owners and listing agents. We operate the website getownerfinancedhomes.com. Our contact details are at the bottom of this page.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit an inquiry form on this site, or call, text, or email us, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, email address, phone number, the property you're asking about, and whatever you write in your message. If you check the text-messaging box, we also record that choice as described in the SMS section below.

Information collected automatically. Our site is served by GitHub Pages, whose servers record standard request logs — IP address, user agent, page requested, and timestamp. We use these only to keep the site running and secure.

What we do not collect. This site sets no cookies of its own, uses no advertising or behavioral tracking pixels, runs no third-party analytics, and stores nothing in your browser's local storage. We do not collect Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, or payment card details through this website, and you should never send them to us by web form, text, or email.

How we use your information

  • To answer your question and follow up on your inquiry.
  • To tell you about properties, showings, offers, and transaction updates relevant to what you asked about.
  • To evaluate a property you have offered to sell us.
  • To keep records of consent, as required by mobile carriers and applicable law.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • To meet our legal, tax, and recordkeeping obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not rent or trade it to anyone for their own marketing.

SMS / Text Messaging

Americas Best Home Solutions LLC offers text messaging to individuals who have provided express written or verbal consent to receive them.

Information collected. When you opt in to text messaging, we collect your mobile phone number, the date and time of your consent, your IP address if consent was given through our website, and the content of messages exchanged with us.

How we use it. We use your mobile number to send information about properties, showings, offers, transaction updates, and follow-up communications relating to your inquiry or our business relationship.

Sharing. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. We share mobile numbers only with the messaging service providers who deliver our messages on our behalf, and only for that purpose.

Opting out. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation message and then no further messages. Reply HELP or contact us at admin@americasbesthomesolutions.com or (551) 550-9906 for assistance.

Rates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Our full messaging program terms are on the Messaging Terms page.

Cookies and tracking

We do not set cookies on this website, and we do not use tracking pixels, advertising tags, session replay, or cross-site tracking of any kind. Because we set no cookies, there is no cookie banner and nothing for you to opt out of here.

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal. Since we do not track visitors across sites, there is no cross-site tracking for those signals to switch off.

Analytics

We do not use Google Analytics or any comparable third-party analytics product on this site. The only usage information available to us is the standard web server log described above, kept by our hosting provider.

Third-party services we use

We keep this list short on purpose. These are the outside services involved in running the site, and what each one receives:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) — hosts the website. Receives your IP address and request details in its server logs.
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) — serves the site's typefaces. Your browser requests font files directly from Google, which means Google receives your IP address and user agent for those requests.
  • Formspree — delivers inquiry form submissions to our inbox. Receives everything you type into the form, plus the IP address your submission came from.
  • Make.com — passes inquiry form submissions into our internal customer records. Receives the same form contents.
  • ipify — a simple IP-lookup service. When an inquiry form is submitted, your browser asks ipify for your public IP address so that we can store it as part of the consent record described above. ipify receives the request; it does not receive your name, email, phone number, or message.
  • Messaging service providers — deliver our text messages. They receive mobile numbers and message content only for that purpose, as described in the SMS section.

Some photographs on this site are exterior street-level images sourced from Google Street View and are labeled as such where they appear. Those images are stored on our own site, so viewing them does not send a request to Google.

How we share information

We share your information only in these situations:

  • Service providers. The vendors listed above, strictly to perform their function for us.
  • Transaction participants. If you move forward on a property, the parties needed to close it — such as a title company, closing attorney, or the property's seller — receive what they need for that transaction.
  • Legal requirements. When we are required to by law, subpoena, or court order, or where necessary to protect our rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfer. If the company is sold or merged, customer records may transfer as part of that transaction.

Mobile phone numbers and text-messaging opt-in data are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes, without exception.

Data retention

We keep inquiry records for as long as we are working with you and for a reasonable period afterward, generally up to three years from our last contact, so we can pick up where we left off if you come back to us.

Text-messaging consent records — the number, the timestamp, the IP address, and the exact wording you agreed to — are kept for at least four years after you opt out, because carriers and regulators can ask us to produce that proof after the fact. Records we are required to keep for tax, contract, or legal reasons are kept for as long as that obligation lasts.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and form submissions are encrypted in transit. We limit access to inquiry records to the people in our business who need them. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so please do not send us sensitive identifiers such as Social Security or bank account numbers through this website.

Your rights and choices

  • Access. Ask us what information we hold about you and we will tell you.
  • Correction. Ask us to fix anything that is wrong.
  • Deletion. Ask us to delete your information. We will do so except where we are required to keep it — most notably text-messaging consent records, which we must retain as proof of opt-in.
  • Opt out of texts. Reply STOP to any message, or ask us directly. This is always free and never affects anything else.
  • Opt out of email or calls. Tell us and we will stop.
  • No sale of data. We do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of on that front.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional statutory rights. Write to us at admin@americasbesthomesolutions.com and we will honor any right that applies to you. We do not charge for these requests and we will not treat you differently for making one.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Material changes to how we handle text messaging will also be reflected on the Messaging Terms page.
