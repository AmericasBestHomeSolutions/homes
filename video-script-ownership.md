# America's Best Home Solutions — "Your Own Front Door" Video Script

**Purpose:** Build trust. Explain the program simply. Move a renter emotionally from "stuck" to "there's a real path."
**Length:** ~90 seconds (≈220 words spoken). Record straight to camera, warm and unhurried.
**Voice:** Joe — first person, calm, plainspoken. You're a neighbor who figured something out, not a salesman.

> NLP notes are in *(italics)* — they're cues for delivery, not lines to read.

---

### SCRIPT

**[Open — look straight into the lens, slight pause before you speak]**

Let me ask you something.

*(pause — let them answer in their head)*

How many rent checks have you written… that you're never going to see again?

**[Warm, lean in a little]**

I'm Joe, with America's Best Home Solutions. And I want to talk to you about the difference between *paying* for a home… and *owning* one.

*(Slow down on "paying" vs "owning" — mark the contrast with your hand.)*

**[Steady, honest]**

When you rent, every month the landlord can raise it. He can decide not to renew. He can sell it out from under you — and there goes your kids' school, your neighbors, your whole life. You did everything right, and you still don't control the roof over your head.

*(Let that land. Don't rush.)*

**[Shift up — this is the turn, bring energy and hope]**

Now picture the same house… but it's *yours*.

The payment doesn't jump every year. Nobody can tell you to leave as long as you hold up your end. You want to paint the kitchen? Plant a tree in the yard? *Go ahead — it's your home.*

And here's the part that changes families: every payment is building something. **Equity.** Something real. Something you can pass down to your kids… or sell one day and walk away with money in your pocket. A renter never gets that. An owner does.

**[Slow, sincere — this is the trust close]**

That's *all* we do. We help hardworking families who can't get a normal bank loan… become homeowners anyway — through seller financing. Honest terms. No games.

**[Warm, direct — soft call to action]**

So take a look at the homes we've got available. Find one that feels right for your family.

And when you're ready… let's talk.

*(Small nod. Half-smile. Hold one beat, then cut.)*

---

### WHY THIS WORKS (the NLP under the hood)
- **Opens with a question, not a pitch** — pulls them into their own experience before you say who you are (lowers the sales guard).
- **"Pay vs. own" contrast frame** — the whole video hangs on one clean either/or, so it's easy to remember and repeat.
- **Loss → relief → gain sequence** — name the pain of renting (loss), flip to security (relief), then the upside of equity (gain). Emotion moves in that order for a reason.
- **"It's your home" repeated** — an embedded ownership suggestion; you're rehearsing the feeling for them.
- **Future pacing** — "picture the same house… but it's yours" makes them mentally try it on.
- **Honesty markers** — "No games," "honest terms," first-person "all we do" — trust is built by *narrowing* the claim, not inflating it.
- **Soft close** — "when you're ready, let's talk" gives control back to them, which paradoxically increases action.

### PRODUCTION TIPS
- Shoot at a real house or a warm room — not a bare office. A front door or porch in the background reinforces the message.
- Good daylight, camera at eye level, phone is fine. Clean audio matters more than 4K.
- One honest take beats ten polished ones. If you fumble a word but the feeling's right — keep it.
- End card: your logo, phone **551-358-6625**, and a "Browse Available Homes" button linking to the homes page.
